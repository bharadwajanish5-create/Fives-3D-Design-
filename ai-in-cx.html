THE CORE PLATFORM

Five products, one CX platform

CommX, AssistX, InsightX, CrmX and SmartX work together across the customer journey. Select a product to read more on CXFirst.ai.

CommX

CommX is an AI-powered conversation intelligence platform built to transform customer engagement at scale. From voice and chat to messaging and digital support channels, CommX helps businesses automate interactions, accelerate response times, and deliver seamless, human-like customer experiences, all with complete visibility and control.
100+ languages·Omnichannel

AssistX

AssistX is an AI-powered Agent Assist platform designed to help modern support teams resolve queries faster, deliver sharper customer conversations, and maintain consistent service quality at scale.
20%+ agent productivity·Live guidance

InsightX

InsightX is an AI-powered contact center analytics platform designed to give businesses real-time visibility into customer conversations, agent performance, operational inefficiencies, and service quality, enabling faster decisions, smarter support operations, and better customer experiences at scale
100% interactions monitored·Voice analytics

CrmX

CrmX is AI-native ticketing engine, designed to move customer requests beyond the contact centre and into the wider organisation. It connects workflows, teams and existing business systems to coordinate actions and support complex resolutions. CrmX helps businesses manage requests, route work and drive resolution across front- and back-office operations
40% faster resolution·API-first

SmartX

Simplify complex business processes with SmartX, CXFirst.ai’s rule-based workflow automation engine. Design, orchestrate and automate multi-stage workflows across teams, systems and communication channels through a simple, visual, no-code interface.
No-code designer·Closed-loop tracking
